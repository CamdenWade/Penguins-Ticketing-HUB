# Stage 5A model card — Penguins historical attendance backtest

## Intended use

An educational business analytics portfolio demonstration of game-level **published attendance** prediction for Pittsburgh Penguins games at PPG Paints Arena, using only official NHL game history and schedule-known features.

## Provenance, observation unit and exclusions

- **Observed target:** `reported_attendance`, from NHL official game-summary reports (not tickets sold, capacity utilization, revenue, turnstile count, or customer purchasing).
- **Population:** 122 regular-season Penguins home games played at PPG Paints Arena over 2023–24, 2024–25 and 2025–26.
- **Excluded:** 2025-11-16 designated Penguins home game at Avicii Arena, Stockholm (not the Pittsburgh arena).
- **Synthetic records:** None used for model input, parameter fitting, evaluation, model selection, or reported accuracy.

## Model families

1. **Historical mean:** predicted attendance = mean of development-season training outcomes. Transparent intercept-only benchmark.
2. **Ridge regression:** interpretable linear combination of standardized schedule-known features. Fits an unpenalized intercept and coefficients minimizing mean squared error plus λ times coefficient squared magnitude. λ from a pre-declared grid.

## Allowed predictors

See `stage5a_feature_dictionary.md`: weekend, Friday, Metropolitan Division matchup indicator, cyclic calendar month, home-game ordinal progress, days since prior PPG home game and home opener flag. These are computed from known schedule information. No game outcome or synthetic purchase fields.

## Temporal evaluation and selection

1. Fit initial candidates on 2023–24 (41 games).
2. Evaluate historical mean and six ridge lambdas on 2024–25 (41 games). Choose λ by validation MAE (ties: RMSE then smaller lambda).
3. Select ridge only if it improves validation MAE by **at least 50 fans per game**; otherwise retain baseline. This is a predefined, conservative heuristic, not a formal significance test.
4. Refit both models on the first 82 games (2023–24 plus 2024–25) using the chosen λ for ridge.
5. Test **once** on 2025–26's 40 PPG games. Report both models and identify the model chosen in step 3.

For transparency, all test results are reported even when the unselected model happens to outperform the chosen model. Model selection **must not** be revised after seeing the 2025–26 results.

## Reproducibility and audit

- Deterministic algorithm, no random draws, no cross-season target leakage.
- Predictors standardized using each fitted training sample only.
- Source RDS MD5 checksum stored in model bundle and verified later.
- Saved predictions recomputed from the persisted model during preflight.
- Exact training/test game IDs, source attribution, candidate penalty grid, and selection rule persisted in bundle.
- Independent preflight checks split boundaries, source consistency, evaluation arithmetic, predictor whitelist, MD5, and no synthetic columns.

## Limitations

- n=82 development games and n=40 test games is small. One season's holdout can differ materially from another season.
- The initial model contains no public or private data on marketing campaigns, ticket giveaways, weather, in-season player availability, or live box office behavior.
- Unmodeled structural changes and nonlinear effects can cause sizable errors.
- `is_metro_opponent` is a division-context heuristic, **not** a quantified causal fan-demand effect.
- `days_since_previous_home` measures time since *previous PPG Paints Arena home game*, not total NHL team rest days.
- Predicted reported attendance can exceed the venue's nominal 18,187 seat reference; predictions are not artificially clipped.
- Historical backtests do **not** establish accuracy for future NHL schedules.
- Group error reports with very few games are descriptive, not rigorous subgroup reliability estimates.

## How to present in an interview

> “I validated three seasons of official NHL attendance, engineered only pregame schedule features, used an earlier season to tune a simple regularized model, and held out the most recent season for an honest comparison against a historical-average baseline. I report errors in fans per game rather than claiming the model has production-grade forecasting accuracy. Synthetic ticket revenue is kept entirely separate from this real-data model.”
